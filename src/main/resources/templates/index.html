About this incident map

This map shows emergency calls attended by the Winnipeg Fire Paramedic Service, drawn from the incident feed WFPS has published from its computer-aided dispatch system since August 2023. It reads the same data the City publishes and refreshes every five minutes. It is an independent project, not affiliated with WFPS or the City of Winnipeg.

What the markers show — and what they do not

The published data locates a call to a neighbourhood, never an address. Each marker sits at an offset from its neighbourhood’s centre so that several calls in one area stay legible, constrained to remain inside that neighbourhood’s boundary. Read a marker as somewhere in this neighbourhood and nothing finer. Calls the feed reports as Outside Winnipeg or Unverified have no usable location and are never plotted.

Active and recently closed calls

A call is active while units remain assigned to it. Once it closes, this map keeps it visible for a short window — marked Closed, with the time units spent on scene — before it drops off. The City’s own page shows active calls only.

How calls are grouped

WFPS publishes a free-text incident type. The three colours here — Fire Rescue, Medical Response and Other — are this site’s own grouping for readability, not an official classification. The exact published type always appears in the table.

Common questions

Is there a fire in Winnipeg right now?

This page lists every call the Winnipeg Fire Paramedic Service currently has units assigned to, including fire rescue calls, medical responses and other emergency dispatches. The count at the top of the page is the live total and the map shows which neighbourhood each call is in. It is rebuilt from the City of Winnipeg’s published dispatch data every minute.

Where is the fire in Winnipeg right now?

The published data locates each call to a neighbourhood, never to a street address, so this map can show which neighbourhood a call is in and nothing finer. Markers are drawn at an offset inside the correct neighbourhood boundary so several calls in one area stay legible. Read a marker as somewhere in this neighbourhood.

Why are there so many sirens near me right now?

Check the map for calls in your part of Winnipeg. Note that this map covers the Winnipeg Fire Paramedic Service only, so sirens you hear may belong to a call that never appears here.

Does this show Winnipeg police calls?

No. The City of Winnipeg publishes dispatch data for the Winnipeg Fire Paramedic Service only. Winnipeg Police Service incidents are not in the feed this site reads and are never shown here.

Is this PulsePoint?

No. PulsePoint is a separate service operated by the PulsePoint Foundation and is not affiliated with this site. This is an independent map built from the City of Winnipeg’s own published open data, covering Winnipeg Fire Paramedic Service calls. More on how the two differ.
